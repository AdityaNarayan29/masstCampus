Features

FAQ

Questions schools ask us.

How long does it take to set up?

Creating your school takes a few minutes. You can add students one by one or import them in bulk from our CSV template.

How do parents stay updated?

Parents sign in to the Masst Campus app to follow attendance, fees and messages from the school. SMS and WhatsApp alerts are coming soon.

Is our data safe?

Each school’s data is kept separate from every other school, access depends on each person’s role, and every change is recorded in an audit log.

What if we need help?

You talk directly to the people building Masst Campus. Reach out any time and we’ll help you get set up.

How much does it cost?

We’re setting pricing together with our first schools. Talk to us about your school and we’ll share a plan that fits.
